Courrier privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026.

Courrier is a mail client that runs on your devices. When you sign in with Google, it asks for access to your Gmail account (the gmail.modify scope) so that it can read, organise and send your mail on your behalf.

What Courrier does with your data

  • It connects only to Google's servers. Your mail never goes to any other server, including one run by Courrier's developer.
  • It keeps a cache of your mail on your device so it can show it quickly and offline. Your sign-in token is stored in the device's keychain.
  • It does not collect analytics, does not show ads, and does not sell or share your data with anyone.

Courrier's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Signing out in Courrier, or deleting the app, removes the cache and the token from your device. You can also revoke Courrier's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
